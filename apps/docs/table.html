Table

Tabular data: registries, reports, dashboards. The component styles the native <table> and adds nothing to its semantics — no JS.

Semantic basis

Roles (table, row, columnheader, rowheader, cell), the <caption> association and scope links are native. The caption is required — it doubles as the table's accessible name.

Canonical markup

<table class="ui-table">
  <caption>Quarterly revenue</caption>
  <thead>
    <tr><th scope="col">Region</th>…</tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th scope="row">EMEA</th>…</tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Borders use border-collapse: separate with explicit cell borders: Firefox does not paint borders on sticky headers under collapse, and Chromium has never applied position: sticky to thead/tr — the separate model with sticky on th is the portable form.

data-zebra stripes odd body rows with the subtle surface. Row hover flips to the opposite surface, so the affordance stays visible on zebra tables. Numeric columns take data-align="end" on every cell of the column (header included) — it right-aligns and enables tabular numerals.

.ui-table-wrap is the horizontal scroll container for wide tables; data-sticky pins thead th to the top of the nearest scroll container (the constrained wrap, or the page for unwrapped tables). A scrollable wrap must be keyboard reachable and named: tabindex="0", role="region" and aria-label.

Accessibility

  • <caption> and scope are required — the accessible name and header associations rest on them.
  • A scrollable wrap gets tabindex="0", role="region" and a label so keyboard users can scroll and find it.
  • Hover is never the sole differentiator — rows are separated by hairlines and content.
  • All color pairs are existing APCA-registered pairs (text/text-muted on the surface family); no new tokens.

Do not

  • No <div> grids instead of <table> — header associations and roles are native to real tables.
  • No sticky on thead/tr — only thead th.
  • No variant classes — options are only data-zebra, data-sticky, data-align.
  • No sorting/selection styling in core — those are host states.
